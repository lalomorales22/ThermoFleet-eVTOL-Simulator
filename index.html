Academic Research

Groundbreaking research in thermodynamic computing for autonomous systems

Main Paper

Thermodynamic Computing for Autonomous eVTOL Navigation

ICRA 2026 (International Conference on Robotics and Automation)
Authors et al.

We present ThermoFleet, a novel simulation platform that integrates thermodynamic computing principles with reinforcement learning for autonomous eVTOL training. By treating navigation as energy minimization and using Gibbs sampling for decision making, we achieve 2-3x energy efficiency improvements over traditional approaches while reducing collision rates to <1%.

Workshop Paper

Scaling Multi-Agent Coordination with Gibbs Sampling

ICRA 2026 Workshop on Urban Air Mobility
Authors et al.

We introduce hierarchical coordination strategies using Block Gibbs sampling and mean-field approximations to scale eVTOL fleet coordination to 1,000+ agents. Our approach reduces computational complexity from O(n²) to O(n log n) while maintaining near-optimal solutions through thermodynamic principles.

Dataset Paper

ThermoFleet: A Benchmark for Urban Air Mobility

NeurIPS 2026 Datasets and Benchmarks Track
Authors et al.

We present ThermoFleet, a comprehensive benchmark for urban air mobility research featuring 10,000+ training episodes, diverse scenarios (weather, traffic, failures), real-world city environments, and standardized evaluation metrics. Includes baseline models and leaderboard for reproducible research.
